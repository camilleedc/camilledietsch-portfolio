    About

    Vision & Approach

    The people closest to me describe me as creative and passionate, with a strong drive to bring people together and lead projects. My business studies gave me solid skills in marketing and management, but they did not fully answer my need for creative expression. So I took on project after project alongside my studies and helped create many artistic events.

    Curious and adaptable, I keep refining my creative eye and exploring new worlds. My ambition is to put this creativity to work for projects with a strong identity, from concept to delivery.

    Production MediaCampaign coordination

    As Media Production Assistant at Dior Couture, I help coordinate international advertising campaigns for fashion, accessories, jewellery and watches, from the creative files to the final placement.

    I follow the production and adaptation of content for every channel (press, out-of-home, cinema, television and digital) and make sure creative guidelines are respected and the brand identity stays consistent in every market.

    This role has given me a hands-on understanding of international asset management, of working with creative teams, and of adapting content to different formats and audiences. I also track advertising trends and new formats for the magazine side.

    07

    Shows in DetailIdylls, HEC Fashion Show 2025

    Beyond the logistics, I design each show as a creative world of its own, working on the theme, the narrative and the audience experience. As creative director of the HEC Fashion Show 2025, I imagined a contemporary reinterpretation of Swan Lake in four acts, mixing fashion, classical dance, electronic music and an opera singer, staged in a former bank with made-to-measure swan wings.

    I came up with the theme, the graphic charter and the logo, chose the venue, coordinated all digital communication, produced the photoshoots and built the website. Around the show I created a magazine and T-shirts, a launch cocktail and a pop-up market.
